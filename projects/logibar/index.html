Logibar

Your Logitech battery levels, in the Windows tray.

Download for Windows ↓ Installation steps GitHub source
At a glance Installation Device support

At a glance

  • Battery at a glance. Mouse, headset and keyboard icons with a battery bar.
  • Low battery warnings. Yellow at 30%; red and a charge alert at 15%.
  • Fits your taskbar. Light and dark taskbar support.
  • Local readings. Reads G HUB locally. No account or network connection.

Install

  1. Install Logitech G HUB and connect your devices.
  2. Download logibar.exe.
  3. Double-click the file. The battery icons appear beside the clock.

If the icons are hidden, open the tray overflow and drag them onto the taskbar. Right-click an icon and select Start with Windows to launch it at sign-in.

Device support

Works with mouse, headset and keyboard battery readings saved by G HUB. Shows the newest reading for each category; tooltips flag readings older than an hour.
